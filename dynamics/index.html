Building Excellence Since 2014

Dynamic Events & Tech Management is the evolution of Solid Rigid Interior Decoration — a company established in Dubai in 2014 with a proven track record in fit-out, renovation, construction, interior decoration, MEP works, and project management.

In 2024, a strategic partnership combined decades of technical construction expertise with professional event management and administration leadership.

Today, Dynamic Events & Tech Management delivers integrated solutions covering events, exhibitions, fit-out, construction, MEP works, and project management across the UAE.
