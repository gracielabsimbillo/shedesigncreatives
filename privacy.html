Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information I collect when you visit this website or contact me, why I collect it, and what your rights are under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who I am

This website is run by Shella Simbillo, an executive operations manager. For anything relating to your personal data, email shella@shellasimbillo.com.

What I collect

  • Information you give me: your name, email address, business details and anything else you share when you email me or book a call.
  • Booking information: calls are booked through Calendly, which collects the details you enter in the booking form on my behalf.
  • Website usage data: this site uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, which uses cookies and similar technology to measure visits and the performance of my adverts. It may collect your IP address, device and browser information, and the pages you view.

Why I use it, and my legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange calls. This is in my legitimate interests, or is a step you've asked for before we enter into a contract.
  • To provide services if we work together. This is necessary to perform our contract.
  • To measure and improve my advertising through the Meta Pixel. This is based on your consent.
  • To keep business and tax records. This is necessary to meet a legal obligation.

Who I share it with

I don't sell your data. I share it only with the service providers who help me run my business: Calendly (scheduling), Meta Platforms (advertising measurement), my email provider and my website host. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry emails and booking details for up to 12 months if we don't go on to work together. Client records are kept for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, usually six years after our work ends.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how I use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email me at the address above. I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
